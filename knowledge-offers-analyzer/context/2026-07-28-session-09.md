# Budget stabilization

Production remains enabled; SPEC-005 remains paused pending demonstrated operator profit. The production ledger evidence isolated legacy polling demand rather than SPEC-005: 485 recheck details on 2026-07-28 came from five scored-listing rechecks every ten minutes; 848 cohort averages for 675 new details came from mileage-banded cache cardinality.

Implemented SPEC-010 and ADR-0013: scored listings no longer receive legacy routine rechecks, never-scored recovery is limited to one listing/profile/30 minutes, the benchmark hot path begins with reusable model/year cohorts, and a denied tier-5 benchmark no longer stops the fresh listing queue. Sweep is intentionally unchanged until its coverage/allocation can be reforecasted without damaging disappearance evidence.
