# Valuation sanity guards

Implemented SPEC-011 after an Opel Astra 2004 false high-discount alert. AUTO.RIA percentile-50
(median) now precedes interquartile/arithmetic means. Claimed low mileage cannot raise fair value
without VIN evidence; VIN-evidenced cars aged 15+ cap such uplift at 5%. No new request type or
schema change; each active cohort refreshes once after deployment to bypass the old one-day cache.
ADR-0014 records the trade-off: conservative treatment of genuine but unverified
low-mileage cars until structured real-mileage data exists.
